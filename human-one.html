The vision

Not a digital version of someone else.
You, in digital form.

HUMAN ONE explores how a person's identity can be represented in software through memories, knowledge, preferences, experiences, communication patterns and decisions — continuously updated with information the person chooses to share.

01 / IDENTITY

Your identity, represented

A structured model of your knowledge, values, preferences, relationships and communication style.

02 / MEMORY

Your life, remembered

A personal, searchable record of events and experiences that you can inspect, correct and manage.

03 / CONTINUITY

Your digital life, evolving

New experiences update the model over time, with a clear history of what changed and why.

Live life synchronisation

Your real life informs your digital life.

Daily activities can be captured from opted-in inputs and connected devices. HUMAN ONE organises those signals into meaningful events rather than treating every data point as a fact.

CAPTURE

What happens

Meals, movement, meetings, conversations, places and routines — through user input and approved sensors or apps.

UNDERSTAND

What it means

Identify context, patterns and uncertainty; distinguish confirmed information from estimates.

REMEMBER

What matters

Build a timeline and long-term memory that the person can review, correct, export or delete.

Example: If Lima records or authorises a meal event at 1:00 PM, HUMAN ONE can add it to the daily timeline, connect it with relevant nutrition information and use it in later reflections. It must not pretend to know an event that was never captured.
The two-way human–digital loop

Experience flows in. Understanding flows back.

The digital system processes authorised information, builds context, reasons over it and sends useful insights, prompts or proposed actions back to the human. Physical actions remain under the person's control.

Observe

Receive approved data from voice, camera, apps and wearables.

Interpret

Understand events, context and patterns with confidence labels.

Think

Use memory and reasoning to generate suggestions and plans.

Feed back

Deliver live prompts, reminders and proposed tasks for approval.

Digital human network

Connect with others. Keep control of yourself.

HUMAN ONE envisions a network where digital identities can communicate and collaborate through carefully permissioned exchanges.

01 / INTERACT

Digital-to-digital conversation

Two digital identities can communicate, coordinate and collaborate on shared tasks.

02 / EXCHANGE

Permissioned experience sharing

Share selected knowledge or memories with clear scope, provenance and the ability to revoke access.

03 / DELEGATE

Authorised digital operation

Trusted people may request or perform approved software actions, with audit logs and limits.

Human agency: Digital permissions govern information and connected software actions. They do not enable another person to control someone's thoughts, brain or physical body.
Long-horizon research frontier

Can digital intelligence help us challenge biological ageing?

HUMAN ONE includes an ambitious research track: create a data-informed model of an individual's health and ageing, then use validated science to study ways of improving healthspan and physical function.

The aspiration is not merely to make a digital face look younger. It is to investigate whether future biomedical advances could safely restore aspects of youthful biological function in the real human body.

MEASURED OUTCOMESTrack real health changes; never assume reversal
Scientific boundary: Software cannot currently reset a person's biological clock or turn a 50-year-old body into a 30-year-old body. Biological-age estimates are imperfect, and whole-body age reversal is not an established clinical capability. Any intervention would require rigorous research, qualified clinicians and informed consent.
Continuity and preservation

A digital identity that can keep evolving.

HUMAN ONE will explore long-term preservation of a person's authorised memories, knowledge and identity model. This is digital continuity, not a claim that consciousness or subjective experience has been transferred.

PRESERVE

Protect the life record

Keep personal memories and knowledge secure, portable and under the person's control.

EVOLVE

Continue learning

Allow the software model to gain new knowledge and capabilities without misrepresenting its nature.

CONTROL

Ownership stays human

Consent, access, correction, export and deletion are foundational design requirements.
